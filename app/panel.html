HOW IT WORKS
Reads your voice channel from the Discord app on this PC (official local RPC,
StreamKit app id). No mods, no bot, no account risk. First run: click
Authorize in Discord once.

TIPS
· turn off Discord's own overlay Voice widget to avoid doubles
· games need borderless / windowed mode
· LIVE + soundboard aren't exposed by Discord's local API
